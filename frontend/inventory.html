How Daily Inventory Works

Opening stock represents yesterday's remaining products carried into the selected day. New Order represents products received on the selected date. Today's remaining stock is the closing stock and becomes tomorrow's opening.

Total inventory is calculated automatically:

Yesterday's Remaining + Today's New Order = Current Inventory
